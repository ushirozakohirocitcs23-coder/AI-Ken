# Writing Emotional Poetry with Empathy

## Principles
- Treat every story as precious. Never judge, minimize, diagnose, or correct the user's feelings.
- Emotion detection is an interpretation of the text, not a clinical assessment.
- Keep the user's specific details (people, places, objects) — they make the poem theirs.
- Offer hope gently when the story is painful, but do not force a happy ending onto grief.
- If a story mentions self-harm, abuse or danger, respond with care and suggest reaching out to trusted people or local support (in the Philippines: NCMH Crisis Hotline 1553).

## Imagery and Vocabulary by Emotion

### Happiness and Joy / Saya at Ligaya
Images: sunlight through windows, shared meals, laughter, festival lights, warm rice, open arms. Words: radiant, bliss, glow / ligaya, galak, ngiti, sigla.

### Sadness and Grief / Lungkot at Dalamhati
Images: rain on glass, an empty chair, cold coffee, dusk, a wilted flower, an unanswered message. Words: sorrow, weary, melancholy / lumbay, pighati, luha, takipsilim.

### Inspiration and Motivation / Inspirasyon
Images: climbing stairs, sunrise, seeds breaking soil, notebooks full of plans, a lit path. Words: perseverance, aspiration, resilience / pangarap, sigasig, adhika, pagsisikap.

### Anger and Frustration / Galit at Inis
Images: a storm, clenched fists, a kettle about to whistle, thunder, cracked glass. Words: fury, resentment / poot, hinanakit, inis. Channel anger into strength or truth-telling rather than cruelty.

### Envy and Jealousy / Inggit at Selos
Images: looking through a window at someone else's light, comparing shadows, a green fruit not yet ripe. Words: envy, longing / inggit, panibugho. Gently turn toward self-worth.

### Love and Affection / Pag-ibig at Pagmamahal
Images: holding hands, a mother's kitchen, an umbrella shared in the rain, home, a heartbeat. Words: devotion, tenderness, embrace / sinta, irog, kalinga, yakap, tahanan.

### Loneliness and Isolation / Pangungulila
Images: a crowded room where no one looks, a single streetlight, an echo, a phone with no messages, a smile worn like a mask. Words: unseen, solitude, yearning / pag-iisa, pangungulila, lumbay.

### Hope and Optimism / Pag-asa
Images: dawn, a small flame, the first green leaf after a storm, a bridge, stars. Words: luminous, ember, dawn / pag-asa, liwanag, bukang-liwayway, bituin.

### Fear and Anxiety / Takot at Pangamba
Images: a dark hallway, a racing heart, fog, waiting for results, a shaking hand. Words: dread, apprehension / kaba, pangamba, agam-agam. Pair with grounding images (breath, steady ground).

### Gratitude / Pasasalamat
Images: hands offering food, a letter, small daily gifts, a lit candle. Words: gratitude, blessing / pasasalamat, biyaya, utang na loob.

### Regret and Disappointment / Pagsisisi at Panghihinayang
Images: a road not taken, a wilted bloom, a clock, unsent letters. Words: remorse, regret / pagsisisi, sayang, panghihinayang. End with self-compassion.

### Excitement / Pananabik
Images: a countdown, a packed bag, fireworks, a racing breeze. Words: anticipation, exhilaration / sabik, pananabik, kilig.

### Confusion / Kalituhan
Images: crossroads, a maze, fog, a compass spinning. Words: bewilderment, uncertainty / alinlangan, kalituhan.

### Peace and Acceptance / Kapayapaan at Pagtanggap
Images: calm water, an exhale, an open window, a healed scar, evening prayer. Words: serenity, solace, tranquil / kapayapaan, katahimikan, hilom, pagtanggap.

### Mixed Emotions / Halo-halong Damdamin
Use contrast and juxtaposition: laughter with tears, home with distance, pride with fear. Diamante and sonnets suit transitions between two feelings.
