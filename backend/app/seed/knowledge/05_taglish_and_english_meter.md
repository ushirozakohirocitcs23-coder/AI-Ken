# Taglish (Filipino-English) Poetry Style Guide
- Code-switch the way Filipinos naturally speak: Filipino grammar backbone with English words for modern or emotionally precise ideas ("Pagod na ang puso ko, but I keep on trying").
- Switch at phrase boundaries, not in the middle of fixed expressions.
- Use English for school, work, tech and pop-culture terms when that is how the user wrote them; keep family terms and feelings in Filipino when the user did ("Nanay," "Kuya," "tampo," "kilig," "hiya").
- Some Filipino emotion words have no exact English equivalent: kilig (giddy romantic thrill), tampo (affectionate sulking), hiya (shame/propriety), gigil (overwhelming urge from cuteness), lambing (affectionate sweetness), sayang (regret over waste). Keep them untranslated.
- Never translate every phrase literally; preserve the emotional register.
- Syllable counting in Taglish: count Filipino words by vowels and English words by pronunciation.

# English Rhyme
- Perfect rhyme: identical stressed vowel and following sounds (light / night; unseen / scene).
- Slant (near) rhyme: similar but not identical sounds (heart / hurt; soul / all). Acceptable in modern poems; traditional forms prefer perfect rhyme.
- Masculine rhyme ends on a stressed syllable (bright / night); feminine rhyme on unstressed (trying / crying).
- Eye rhyme looks alike but sounds different (love / move) — avoid when a strict rhyme is required.

# English Meter
- Iamb: unstressed + stressed (da-DUM), e.g. "a-WAY." Iambic pentameter = five iambs, about 10 syllables: "I WEAR a SMILE be-NEATH the GOLD-en LIGHT."
- Trochee: DUM-da. Anapest: da-da-DUM (limericks). Dactyl: DUM-da-da.
- Shakespearean sonnets use iambic pentameter; limericks use anapestic lines (3 beats in lines 1, 2, 5; 2 beats in lines 3, 4).
- Haiku and tanka in English count syllables, not stresses.

# Revision Checklist
1. Is every required line present and in order?
2. Does each line meet the syllable target?
3. Do the end words follow the rhyme scheme?
4. Does the poem still tell the user's story and honor their emotion?
5. Is every vocabulary word used naturally and correctly?
